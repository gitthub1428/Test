QuizPilot
  1. Press Ctrl + Shift + B to show your bookmarks bar.
  2. Drag the green button onto the bookmarks bar. Don't click it; drag it.
  3. Open your practice quiz and click the QuizPilot bookmark.
  4. Optional: press Ctrl + Shift + J to watch the progress and see the report.

It fills the answers it is confident about and never submits. Check the answers, then submit yourself.
